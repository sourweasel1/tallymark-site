Your money data lives on your phone. Nowhere else.

Tallymark is a money tracker in development for iPhone: balances, spending, budgets, and alerts — with no Tallymark server and no account. Your transactions are stored only on your device. We never see them, because there's nowhere for them to go.

The model: buy the app once — $9.99* — and, if you want automatic bank sync, bring your own SimpleFIN Bridge subscription ($15/year + tax, paid to SimpleFIN, covers up to 25 institutions). No subscription to us. Ever.

*The one-time price for the public version, if it's built. SimpleFIN is optional: the app works fully without it (see below).

How it works

There's no Tallymark cloud, so setup looks a little different from apps that hold your bank login on their servers. It takes about 10 minutes, once.

1

Buy the app once

One purchase from the App Store ($9.99). No subscription, no account to create with us, nothing to renew.

2

Get your own sync

If you want automatic updates, subscribe to SimpleFIN Bridge yourself — $15/year + tax, paid directly to SimpleFIN. Your bank connections live there, under your account, revocable by you at any time.

3

Connect banks there, paste one token

Link your banks on SimpleFIN's site, then paste a one-time setup token into Tallymark. The app talks to SimpleFIN directly from your phone. New transactions land in a database that exists only on your device.

What we don't claim

"Private" gets stretched a lot in this category. Here is exactly where our claim ends:

  • SimpleFIN and MX see your data. SimpleFIN's connections run through its upstream provider, MX. They hold your bank connection and see data in transit — that's how the sync works. Our promise is narrower and true: the app developer stores nothing, because there is no developer server. You choose the sync provider, you pay them, and you can revoke the connection whenever you like.
  • Sync is roughly daily, not real-time. SimpleFIN Bridge is built for daily updates. A charge you just made may not appear until tomorrow. If you need minute-by-minute freshness, this model isn't for you.
  • The app works without sync. Manual entry and file import are built in, so if SimpleFIN ever shuts down or you drop it, Tallymark keeps working and your history stays on your phone. You're never locked in to the sync provider to keep your own records.
  • No bank credentials touch the app. You enter them only on SimpleFIN's side. Tallymark holds a revocable access token in your phone's Keychain — never your bank password.

What's in the app

Tallymark already exists as a working private app. The public version would ship the same core, rebuilt around your own sync:

  • Balances at a glance — every account, bank, card, and loan in one place.
  • Income vs. spending — money in, money out, and a six-month cash-flow view.
  • Categorized transactions — searchable feed; tap to re-categorize, and teach it "always file this merchant this way."
  • Monthly budgets — per category, with recurring charges surfaced separately.
  • Unusual-purchase alerts — first-time merchants and large one-offs flagged; mark one "looks fine" and it learns.
  • Mortgage done right — loan payments counted once, as mortgage — never mistaken for income.
